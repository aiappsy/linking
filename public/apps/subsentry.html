How does SubSentry detect deceptive subscription dark patterns? +

SubSentry analyzes checkout workflows and SaaS terms of service in real time, scoring vendors on subscription transparency and alerting you to forced annual billing traps, hidden phone-in cancellation clauses, and automatic price hikes before you enter your credit card.

What is the 1-Click Cancellation Playbook CMS? +

Instead of navigating through convoluted settings menus or waiting in live support chat queues, SubSentry provides verified, direct cancellation links and legally compliant cancellation templates for hundreds of popular cloud and consumer SaaS applications.

How does the Intelligent Alternative Switch Engine work? +

When you mark an overpriced or redundant tool for cancellation, SubSentry suggests modern, privacy-focused, or open-source software alternatives that offer similar or superior capabilities at significantly lower costs.
