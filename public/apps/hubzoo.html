Frequently Asked Questions

What is Hubzoo and how does it work? +

Hubzoo is an autonomous AI sales assistant and lightweight CRM built specifically for tradespeople, contractors, and busy consultants. It enables you to draft customer-ready proposals on your mobile phone in under 60 seconds while standing on-site, and automatically schedules thoughtful, human-sounding follow-ups via WhatsApp, SMS, and Email until the deal is closed.

Does Hubzoo integrate with Norwegian accounting engines like Fiken and Tripletex? +

Yes. Hubzoo includes native, two-way automated webhooks for Fiken and Tripletex. The instant a customer approves your quote on their smartphone with one tap, the customer record and invoice rows sync directly into your bookkeeping software without manual re-entry.

How does the autonomous follow-up work without feeling spammy? +

Hubzoo utilizes Gemini AI language models to produce context-aware, respectful follow-up messages tailored to the specific quote. If an inquiry isn't answered in 48 hours, it sends a friendly check-in. After 5 days, it checks if the client has questions about the timeline or scope, ensuring leads never go cold.
