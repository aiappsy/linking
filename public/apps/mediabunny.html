Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is on-device WebAssembly (WASM) media processing superior? +

Typical web converters force you to upload large media files to remote servers, causing privacy risks, slow transfer speeds, and server queue waits. MediaBunny executes compiled C/C++ WebAssembly binaries directly on your computer or phone CPU, meaning zero upload wait times and complete data confidentiality.

What is EBU R128 audio normalization? +

EBU R128 (ITU-R BS.1770-4) is the international broadcast loudness standard. MediaBunny analyzes and normalizes voice tracks, podcasts, and commercial audio to exact streaming LUFS targets with true-peak dBTP limiting to prevent digital distortion.

How much file size reduction does smart CRF video compression achieve? +

MediaBunny's CRF compression reduces video file sizes by up to 75% with zero perceptible loss in visual clarity, ideal for web hero animations, social media uploads, and email attachments.

Can MediaBunny operate without an internet connection? +

Yes. MediaBunny is built as a Progressive Web App (PWA) with full service-worker caching. Once visited, the core audio, video, and background removal tools can function entirely offline.
