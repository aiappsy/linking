Last updated: September 16, 2026 · Governed by Norwegian & EU Law (GDPR) Sist oppdatert: 16. september 2026 · Underlagt norsk lov og GDPR

Summary in plain English: We collect only what is strictly necessary to evaluate your project inquiries and run our services. We do not sell your data, do not run third-party advertising cookies, and ensure all infrastructure operates in secure, tenant-isolated cloud environments.

1. Data Controller

The data controller responsible for the processing of personal data on aiappsy.com is:

AIAPPSY / Pål Juritzen

Direct Contact: paljuritzen@gmail.com

2. What Information We Collect

  • Project Inquiries: When you submit our contact form, we collect your name, email address, chosen project category, and project scope details.
  • Server Access Logs: Standard HTTP server logs including IP address, browser user-agent, requested URL, and timestamps. These are maintained exclusively for cybersecurity, rate-limiting, and DDoS defense.
  • Local Storage Preferences: We store your preferred interface language (English or Norwegian) in your browser's local storage. This does not track you across other websites.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data under the following legal grounds specified in GDPR Article 6:

  • Contract Scoping (Art. 6(1)(b)): To respond to your project requests and provide technical architectural assessments.
  • Legitimate Interest (Art. 6(1)(f)): To defend against unauthorized automated abuse, malicious traffic, and cyber attacks.

4. Data Sharing & Sub-Processors

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We utilize secure enterprise cloud infrastructure to operate this site:

  • Google Cloud Platform (Cloud Run & Firestore): High-security container hosting with automated isolation.

5. Your Rights Under GDPR

As a data subject located in Norway, the EEA, or internationally, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you (Article 15).
  • Request rectification of incorrect data (Article 16).
  • Request the immediate erasure of your personal data ("Right to be Forgotten", Article 17).
  • Lodge a complaint directly with the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet) at datatilsynet.no.

6. Contact Regarding Privacy

For any data access, rectification, or deletion requests, simply email us at paljuritzen@gmail.com. We respond within 24–48 hours.
